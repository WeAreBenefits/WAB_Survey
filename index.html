COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked
questions.

Does We Are Benefits replace our current health plan?

No. Our focus is connecting employers with supplemental benefit providers whose programs may complement existing benefits.

Is We Are Benefits an insurance carrier?

No. Insurance products, where applicable, should be offered through appropriately licensed individuals and entities. Third-party providers are responsible for their respective products and services.

Are savings guaranteed?

No. Any savings examples are illustrative. Actual results depend on eligibility, plan structure, participation, payroll facts, applicable law, and provider terms.

How does implementation work?

After an initial discovery and provider match, the selected provider handles the applicable program evaluation, administration, enrollment, fulfillment, and servicing.
