ABOUT WE ARE BENEFITS

Better connections.
Better benefit possibilities.

We Are Benefits connects employers with third-party providers of supplemental insurance and employee benefit products and services. We help organizations explore benefit solutions that may strengthen the employee experience while identifying potential payroll efficiencies through appropriately structured programs.

WHAT WE DO

We make benefit opportunities easier to understand and evaluate.

Our role is to help employers navigate the front end of the process: understanding available solutions, connecting with the appropriate providers, and coordinating conversations around workforce needs, payroll structure, implementation, and enrollment.

01

Explore Benefit Options

Identify supplemental health, wellness, insurance, and employee benefit solutions that may fit the workforce.

02

Understand the Structure

Help employers understand how available programs may interact with payroll and, where applicable, Section 125 plan structures.

03

Connect With Providers

Introduce employers to third-party providers and appropriately licensed entities responsible for their respective products and programs.

04

Coordinate the Process

Help keep the path from initial evaluation through provider handoff, implementation, and enrollment clear and organized.

OUR APPROACH

Simple, transparent, and employer-focused.

Every organization is different. We start with the actual workforce and business goals rather than treating every employer as if the same solution will fit.

1

Understand Your Business

We learn about your employee population, current benefits, payroll profile, and objectives.

2

Review the Opportunity

We explore which provider solutions may align with the organization and what questions should be evaluated before moving forward.

3

Coordinate the Handoff

When there is a potential fit, we connect the employer with the appropriate provider and licensed professionals for program-specific evaluation and implementation.

4

Keep Expectations Clear

We avoid guaranteed savings claims and encourage appropriate legal, tax, payroll, benefits, and compliance review when needed.

WHAT MAKES US DIFFERENT

Focused on relationships, clarity, and practical implementation.

We Are Benefits is designed as a connection point between employers and specialized benefit providers—not as a replacement for the professionals responsible for underwriting, administration, enrollment, legal advice, tax advice, or plan servicing.
