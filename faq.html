GENERAL QUESTIONS

What We Are Benefits does.

What does We Are Benefits do?

We connect employers with third-party providers of supplemental insurance and employee benefit products and services, helping employers identify solutions that may fit their workforce.

Is We Are Benefits an insurance company?

No. We Are Benefits is not an insurance carrier. Insurance products, where applicable, are offered through appropriately licensed individuals and entities.

Does this replace our current benefits broker or health plan?

No. Our focus is supplemental solutions that may complement an employer’s existing benefits strategy.

SECTION 125 / TAX QUESTIONS

Plain-English answers about cafeteria plans.

What is a Section 125 cafeteria plan?

Generally, a Section 125 cafeteria plan is an employer-sponsored arrangement that can allow employees to choose among certain qualified benefits and, when requirements are met, pay for eligible benefits on a pre-tax basis. Employers should rely on their plan administrator and qualified tax or legal advisors for their specific arrangement.

Does a Section 125 arrangement guarantee employer savings?

No. Potential payroll tax efficiency depends on the specific plan design, eligible elections, participation, payroll profile, applicable law, and provider requirements.

Can every supplemental benefit be included?

No. Tax treatment varies by benefit and structure. The applicable provider, administrator, and qualified advisors should confirm eligibility and treatment before implementation.

EMPLOYER QUESTIONS

What to expect during evaluation and rollout.

What does an employer need to get started?

Usually an initial discussion about workforce size, payroll profile, current benefits, goals, and the types of supplemental solutions being considered. Specific providers may request additional information.

Will implementation disrupt payroll?

Implementation requirements depend on the selected program. The provider and applicable administrators coordinate the required payroll and enrollment steps with the employer.

Do employees have to participate?

Participation rules depend on the specific program and plan documents. Employers should review those requirements with the provider and administrator.

Does We Are Benefits provide legal or tax advice?

No. Employers should consult qualified legal, tax, payroll, accounting, and insurance professionals regarding their circumstances.

EMPLOYEE QUESTIONS

What employees may see during enrollment.

Will employees see changes on their paycheck?

They may, depending on the program and employee elections. Any payroll deductions, reimbursements, or benefit-related entries should be explained by the provider or plan administrator before enrollment.

Are benefits the same for every employee?

Not necessarily. Eligibility, elections, availability, and coverage can vary by program, provider, location, employment status, and plan terms.

COST & SAVINGS

How to think about potential efficiency.

How much can my business save?

There is no universal amount. Any illustration should be treated as an estimate until the appropriate provider reviews the employer’s actual payroll profile, participation assumptions, plan design, and applicable requirements.

Is this “free money” for employers?

No. Benefits programs involve eligibility requirements, plan rules, provider terms, administration, and compliance obligations. Potential efficiencies should not be treated as guaranteed or risk-free.
